A pill label — Buttery's eyebrow above page titles, plus role/status chips and recipe metadata tags.

```jsx
<Badge variant="secondary">A social recipe box on the open web</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline">vegetarian</Badge>
<Badge>owner</Badge>
```

- `secondary` (butter) is the canonical eyebrow, always with `margin-bottom` above an `.display-title` heading.
- `outline` is the metadata/facet tag (cuisine, diet, yield) and the "you" marker.
- Uppercase micro-chips ("soon", "priority") use `variant="outline"` at `0.6rem` with letter-spacing.
