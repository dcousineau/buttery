# Ecosystem

Log of relevant sites in the atproto + recipes ecosystem. Anything we look to in the atmosphere for inspiration goes here.

> **Convention:** Any time we reference an app in the atmosphere for inspiration, add it to this log.

## Sites

- **[recipe.exchange](https://recipe.exchange/)** — atproto-native recipe sharing. Our primary reference point.
- **[pantryhost.app](https://pantryhost.app/)** — recipe app in the atmosphere by [@jpdevries.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/jpdevries.bsky.social).
- **[arecipe.app](https://arecipe.app/)** — recipe app in the atmosphere by [@chase523.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/chase523.bsky.social).
- **[eatyourbooks.com](https://www.eatyourbooks.com/)** — an index of recipes in cookbooks and food magazines/sites: searchable by ingredient, title, and book, with reviews and bookmarking, but it indexes recipes rather than hosting their text. See [`docs/resources/EATYOURBOOKS.md`](resources/EATYOURBOOKS.md) for the data-access investigation.
